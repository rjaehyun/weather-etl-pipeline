   # Weather Analytics Queries

   ## 1. Average Temperature Per City
   Average high and low temperatures, plus number of days recorded.

   ## 2. Hottest and Coldest Days
   Extremes per city with dates.

   ## 3. Total Rainfall Per City
   Sum of rainfall and number of rainy days.

   ## 4. Heavy Rain Days
   Days with rainfall > 10mm.

   ## 5. Temperature Range
   Daily high-low spread per city.

   ## 6. City Comparison
   All cities side by side for the latest date.

   ## 7. 7-Day Moving Average
   Advanced: moving average of max temps using window functions.

   ## 8. Data Quality Check
   Validate for nulls and logical errors.
